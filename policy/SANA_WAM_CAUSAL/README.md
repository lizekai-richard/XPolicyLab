# SANA_WAM_CAUSAL

**Contributor:** NVIDIA (zekail) | **Paper:** SANA-WAM (to appear) | **arXiv:** pending | **Original code:** Sana (NVlabs), internal RoboDojo chunk-causal policy line (branch `rwm/zekai-merge`)

`SANA_WAM_CAUSAL` adapts the SANA **chunk-causal policy** `SanaRWMVideoQwenNextSubAttnResV2SelfFlowWorldModelCameraConditionMultiViewCausal_5B_P1_D36` to XPolicyLab. It is the 32-layer hybrid trunk of the bidirectional SANA_WAM policy made causal over action chunks. The 24 Gated DeltaNet layers carry a chunk-level delta-rule recurrence from chunk to chunk. The 8 softmax layers (3, 7, ..., 31) attend over the episode's first observation (the **sink**) plus a sliding window of the newest committed chunks. The model trains with **two-stream teacher forcing** over whole episodes and is deployed the same way: the memory opens at the episode's first frame with the sink (and, for a `padded` window, a **frozen first chunk**: that frame held for one chunk), every chunk is generated against the memory with no observation latent of its own -- it reads its current frame from the memory, the last frame of the most recent memory latent (the sink for chunk 0) -- and after it executes, its observed outcome (camera frames encoded on through one causal VAE stream per view; under `memory_content: video_and_action` also the commands actually sent and the chunk-start state) is committed into memory before the next chunk is generated. This is the **episode protocol** of Sana `d37817788` / `fcccb4c88` / `3cbe3d85e` (training) and `4bd1b674c` (deploy) in its **single form** since Sana `4bd6eca83` (2026-10-14: chunk observations retired, the canvas [sink | clean 0..L-2 | noisy targets 0..L-1] for every L, the deploy's `generate_chunk` without an observation). Checkpoints trained with chunk observations (`chunk_observation: true`) are refused; this adapter served them up to XPolicyLab `d57a617`. Checkpoints of the earlier protocols are refused too: the sink-less per-chunk observation protocol (the pantheon14k m48n24 runs), whose adapter is archived as `XPolicyLab_snapshots/SANA_WAM_CAUSAL_per_chunk_obs_pre_episode_20261013` (x5) and `/homes/jiamingt/xpl_zekai/archive/` (NFS), and the chunk-0-only observation layout (the vanilla52k line, this repository's commit `69cfb8e`). Supported: `bench_name=RoboDojo`, `env_cfg_type=arx_x5`, `action_type=ee`. The checkpoint must be the EEF-only absolute `robot_base_eef` line on the three-view `sana_latent` strip (each camera stretched to 256x320, an 8x10 latent per view) or the one-view `sana_pixel` 320x512 canvas, with `rope: aligned` and strided video. The model runs **in-process** through the vendored, Sana-free package `sana_wam_causal/`, a mirror of Sana `rwm/zekai-merge` (checked against `7de7711c2`): `sana_qwennext_policy_causal.py`, `layers/chunk_causal_{gdn,softmax,block}.py`, the inference subset of `deploy/causal/session.py` and `deploy/causal/view_stream.py`. It sits on top of the sibling adapter's runtime `policy/SANA_WAM/sana_wam_min/`, which supplies the bidirectional trunk modules, the Flow-Euler sampler, the LTX-2.3 causal VAE encoder, Gemma-2-2B text conditioning, the strip and canvas front-ends, Robot80 normalization, the EEF kinematics and the RoboDojo codecs. `policy/SANA_WAM` itself is not modified. This is an **eval-only** submission: training and data conversion live in the Sana repository, and `process_data.sh` / `train.sh` print a notice and exit 0.

Shared conventions (argument meanings, checkpoint naming, split-machine deployment, `EVAL_ENV_TYPE`) are documented in the [XPolicyLab README](../../README.md). Official results: [RoboDojo LeaderBoard](https://robodojo-benchmark.com/LeaderBoard).

## Installation

SANA_WAM_CAUSAL runs in the **same environment as SANA_WAM**. `install.sh` installs the same pinned stack into the active environment:

```bash
conda create -n sana_wam python=3.11 && conda activate sana_wam
cd XPolicyLab/policy/SANA_WAM_CAUSAL && bash install.sh
# = torch 2.9.1 / torchvision 0.24.1 (cu128; SANA_WAM_SKIP_TORCH=1 keeps an existing CUDA torch), diffusers>=0.38,
#   transformers>=4.46,<5, safetensors, numpy, pillow, pyyaml, huggingface_hub, and XPolicyLab itself (pip install -e).
```

`policy/SANA_WAM/` must sit next to this directory, because `sana_wam_causal` imports its `sana_wam_min` package (`sana_wam_causal/shared.py` puts it on `sys.path`; the import fails loudly otherwise). No `flash_attn` is needed: Sana's causal cross-attention hard-imports it, whereas this port uses the bidirectional mirror's SDPA cross-attention. The text encoder and VAE weights are not part of the checkpoint. `deploy.yml` points `text_encoder_path` / `vae_path` at copies of `google/gemma-2-2b-it` and `Efficient-Large-Model/LTX-2.3-Diffusers`; when the keys are null, `SANA_WAM_TEXT_ENCODER_PATH` / `SANA_WAM_VAE_PATH` are read.

## Data Processing

Not supported in this adapter (eval-only). The training dataset is Sana's `CausalRoboDojoSFTDataset` on **episode phase windows**: every window is a whole clean span of an episode from its first frame, one window per chunk-grid phase h = 1..32 (h frozen copies of the first frame ahead of the span; chunk 0 is memory only, wholly frozen at h = 32), so every row of a span but its last is the observation of exactly one supervised chunk per epoch. The latents come from the phase-window store (`v2-f33-vs4-phase-stretch-ae44f4a5961380ab`): per view ONE causal VAE encode of the whole window gives the sink (its first latent) and every chunk's target latents (the store's per-chunk observation latents are read by no recipe since Sana `4bd6eca83`). A deployment is the phase whose chunk grid lands on the first frame (`aligned_phase_window`). `bash process_data.sh ...` prints a notice and exits 0.

Episode recipe `n24`:

- C = 32 action rows per chunk (1.28 s at 25 Hz).
- `video_fps` 8, i.e. video frame stride 4 and one latent frame per chunk.
- The two-stream canvas [sink | clean targets 0..L-2 | noisy targets 0..L-1] (the sink on every canvas, L >= 1), every noisy chunk its target latents alone, reading its observation from the memory; whole-episode windows of up to 49 chunks; softmax window W = 24 chunks, whose evicting readers take the sink at the frame just before their window (StreamingLLM).
- `memory_content`: what a clean (memory) chunk carries besides its target latent -- `video_and_action` (its anchor state and actions too) or `video_only`.
- `data.extra.aligned_phase_window` (Sana `04c8acbee`, user 2026-10-12): the window of the phase whose chunk grid lands on the first frame. `padded` (absent) opens one chunk ahead of the first frame, its chunk 0 the first frame frozen over the chunk and memory only; `first_frame` opens on the first frame, its chunk 0 supervised and reading the first-observation sink. The deploy follows it: a padded window commits the frozen chunk 0 after the sink and emits from chunk 1; a first-frame window keeps the frozen chunk out of the memory and emits from chunk 0, after the sink. The per-view VAE streams of a first-frame window open on the sink alone, as Sana's deploy does since `9fca24879`; the causal encoder pads with the first frame, so its chunk latents equal the padded stream's up to float32 round-off (the phase store serves that window as the wholly frozen phase's member less its chunk 0; Sana's audit measured the two bit-identical, the CPU stream test sees <= 4e-7). The released `..._episode_n24_nsc_videoonly_noobs` checkpoint trains `first_frame` from step 2074.
- Flow shifts: video 5, action 1. 10k steps, 500-step warmup to 5e-5, cosine to 1e-6.

## Model Assets

Checkpoint layout (the accelerate FSDP full state dict of the causal policy, as the trainer saves it):

```text
<ckpt_dir>/
├── config.yaml                                   # the training yaml: model.model = the causal class above,
│                                                 #   model.extra.chunk_causal_policy (memory_content, sliding_window_chunks),
│                                                 #   data.type CausalRoboDojoSFTDataset
├── model/pytorch_model_fsdp.bin                  # fp32 full state dict: the bidirectional trunk's keys + per GDN block
│                                                 #   attn.gate_proj.{weight,bias}, attn.A_log, attn.dt_bias (attn.recall_gate)
└── normalization/robodojo_arx_x5_model_fps_25_f33_normalization.json   # the f33 Robot80 statistics the yaml pins
```

`config.yaml` may also sit one or two levels above the checkpoint dir, as in the `checkpoints/epoch_X_step_Y/` layout of a training run. `ckpt_name` resolves through `XPolicyLab.utils.checkpoint_resolver.resolve_checkpoint_root` exactly as for SANA_WAM: explicit `checkpoint_dir` / `checkpoint_path` / `ckpt_dir` / `model_dir` first, then `ckpt_name` as a path, then `checkpoints/<bench_name>-<ckpt_name>-<env_cfg_type>-<action_type>-<seed>/`, then `checkpoints/<ckpt_name>/`.

**Contract.** `sana_wam_causal/contract.py` reads everything from the yaml and refuses anything it does not implement, before the weights are read:

- **Geometry.** Actions per chunk come from `model.extra.chunk_causal_policy.actions_per_chunk` at the model fps; the dataset's chunk must agree. The tier is `data.extra.robot_sft.tier_num_frames = C + 1` (33). The video stride comes from `video_fps`. The window is `sliding_window_chunks`. `memory_content` is `video_and_action` (also when the key is absent, as Sana reads it) or `video_only`; `chunk_observation` is absent or false (Sana `refuse_chunk_observation`: anything else, `true` included, is refused). Like Sana, the config alone cannot tell a run from before 2026-10-14 that trained chunk observations without declaring the key (absent meant true then, e.g. the episode VO run `..._episode_n24_nsc_videoonly` and the `0081aa702` `..._episode_n24_nsc` video_and_action recipe). The Hub upload of a causal checkpoint records the run's protocol next to its `config.yaml` (`RUN_ID.json`, copied into `upload_manifest.json`: `chunk_observation`, `aligned_phase_window`, `memory_content`, `source_commit`, `config_sha256`); the runtime reads both before any weight and refuses a record of chunk observations, or one that contradicts the config (`contract.check_run_record`). A repo that ships no record can be given `RUN_ID.local.json` next to its `config.yaml` (written after the download, e.g. the training tree its README names, with a `provenance` line), read the same way. The ready line prints `run_record=` with the keys read and whether the config is the recorded one (`config_sha256=match|differs|unrecorded`), or `none`: a checkpoint without a record (a raw training directory) is served from its config alone, so make sure it was trained without chunk observations. Serve chunk-observation checkpoints from XPolicyLab `d57a617`, never from this one. `data.extra.aligned_phase_window` is `padded` (also when absent) or `first_frame`, which sets the first emitted chunk (1 or 0; the ready line prints `aligned_phase_window` and `first_chunk`).
- **Per-chunk observation latent.** `model.extra.chunk_causal_policy.causal_observation` (Sana `764ee2113`, deploy `adc4867ea`; false when absent) gives every noisy chunk its own observation latent o_c -- its start frame encoded alone, every view's (chunk 0's is the sink's latent) -- clean, one latent frame after the most recent memory latent; it reads the sink, the softmax window, the GDN state and itself, never its chunk's targets or robot rows, and is never committed. `causal_state` (`afc9874b6`, deploy `8b599e49d`; only with `causal_observation`) puts the chunk's state row in o_c's group. The session prefills o_c once per text stream before the denoising steps (`CausalPolicySession.prefill_observation`, Sana `forward_observation`: video frame c f_c + 1, the state row under `causal_state` at step c C + C / f_c); every step reads it besides the window (the softmax its rotated K/V after the window; the GDN the branch that o_c's group wrote into the carried state by the delta rule, in place of the state, under the window's own decay and key scale: Sana `fd5261ff6`, sana-rwm's two-stream obs-block), its targets one frame later (c f_c + 2 ..), its robot rows C / f_c steps later and, under `causal_state`, the C action rows alone. The commit is the policy's without the option. The adapter passes the first emitted chunk the sink's latent and every later chunk its start frame (the frame the previous chunk ended on) encoded alone on fresh streams (`ViewStreams.encode_observation`, the episode's streams untouched; logged as `observation_encode_s`). **GDN observation form.** Up to Sana `fd5261ff6`'s parent every denoise read pooled its decay and key scale with o_c's tokens and read o_c's terms besides the state (`gdn_observed_read`), and o_c wrote nothing; no config key tells the two forms apart, so the run record's training trees decide (`contract.check_observation_form`): a record naming a branch-form tree (`OBSERVATION_BRANCH_TREES`) is served, a record naming only joint-read trees is refused (served by SANA_WAM_CAUSAL up to XPolicyLab `e35fcde`: the mix-v3 N1-obs and the physical-RoPE sana_latent checkpoints), and an observation checkpoint without a known tree is refused unless it is a donor directory; the ready line prints `gdn_observation=`. Only yaml booleans are accepted; a run record that names other values is refused; the ready line prints `causal_observation=` / `causal_state=`; the attention probe refuses an observation policy.
- **Physical RoPE.** `model.extra.chunk_causal_policy.physical_rope` (Sana `6e91eaf2a`; false when absent, `rope: aligned` only) puts every token at its physical instant: a target latent, generated or committed, at the middle of the frames it covers (c f_c + i + 0.5625, `latent_centre_offset` = (8 + 1) / 16), an observation latent at its chunk's start c f_c, the state row and action j at their steps c C and c C + j without the observation shift, the sink at 0. The GDN layers run on that clock; the softmax reads, numbered from their origin, place the sink at the window's start instant (Sana's rotation of the sink to lo f_c), so a one-chunk window reads sink / memory / observation / targets at 0 / 0.5625 / 1 / 1.5625. Sana's own cached session still refuses the option (posttrain_causal_handoff 11, R4); this adapter serves it, checked against Sana's training canvas. The ready line prints `physical_rope=`; a run record (top level or its `deploy` block) naming another value is refused.
- **Without AttnRes.** `model.use_attn_res` (`true` when absent; Sana `53234167c` lets the chunk-causal policy train with `false`) runs the block stack as a plain residual: every layer adds its attention sublayer's delta (self- plus cross-attention) to the hidden state, then its MLP delta, and the last hidden state goes to the final layer and the action head (Sana `_plain_layer_step`); there is no `attn_res` module and no final AttnRes aggregation. The contract carries it (`use_attn_res=` on the ready line); the bidirectional view keeps the shared config's AttnRes pin. The weights must agree: a checkpoint without `attn_res.*` tensors into an AttnRes model, or one with them into a plain model, fails the strict load.
- **Sink horizon (Sana `0c88c11ab` on).** From `0c88c11ab` (yuyang's two-stream pretrain clock, no switch kept) the physical clock is the only one -- `physical_rope` is no longer read and the recipes drop it -- and a softmax reader in chunk c (its observation, its denoising window, its commit) reads the sink at the instant chunk max(0, c - 7) starts (Sana `sink_read_chunks`, `SINK_HORIZON_CHUNKS` = 7) instead of at its window's start: chunks 0..7 read it in place, a later chunk sees it 7 chunks before its own start with its window in between. The GDN keeps the absolute clock. No config key tells the two sink rules apart (a horizon recipe without `physical_rope` would resolve to the latent-slot clock on its own), so the run record's training trees decide (`contract.resolve_rope_clock`, before the record is checked): a tree of `SINK_HORIZON_TREES` (`0c88c11ab`, `e6c386907`, `6cf45c1ae`, `7ed0d28bf`) serves the physical clock with `sink_horizon=7` (the session numbers each read from chunk max(0, c - 7), the sink at frame 0 there), and refuses a softmax window above 7 or none, as Sana does; a tree of `WINDOW_START_SINK_TREES` (the observation trees, `7de7711c2`, `b297028fe`, ...) serves the window-start sink on the clock `physical_rope` declares; records naming only unknown trees are refused (add the tree to one tuple after `git merge-base --is-ancestor 0c88c11ab <tree>`), and so is a checkpoint without a record unless it is a step-0 donor directory. A run resumed onto a horizon tree takes the horizon rule and the label names the earlier segment. The ready line prints `sink_horizon=` in the contract and `rope_clock=` (e.g. `physical+sink_horizon7(0c88c11ab; trained on 7ed0d28bf)` or `physical+sink_window_start(trained on a5ae8cf8f)`).
- **GDN readout.** `model.memory_gdn_output_normalizer` (Sana `c414e9b32`; `true` when absent, so every earlier checkpoint) decides every GDN layer's output: `true` divides the numerator `q (gS + K^T diag(beta) V)` by `ReLU(q) . (gz + K_den^T beta) + eps`, `false` returns the numerator alone (z is still carried, so a commit writes the same state). The per-head RMSNorm after it cancels any positive denominator; the two differ where it is near zero or negative (Sana's audit: degenerate layer-0 heads). The Sana recipes that set it `false` are the sana_latent N1-obs / N2-obs episode recipes, from their segment 7 (`b4de67828`). Only a yaml boolean is accepted (Sana's `bool()` would serve the string `"false"` as `true`); a run record that names a different value is refused; the ready line prints `gdn_output_normalizer=`; the attention probe (`attention_dump_dir`) refuses a numerator-only checkpoint, its GDN parts being shares of the denominator.
- **Refused contracts:** `data.extra.window_repeats` (the sink-less per-chunk observation protocol: the pantheon14k m48n24 runs; no episode recipe sets it, its launcher refuses it); any `obs_in_first_chunk` key (the chunk-0-only layout: the vanilla52k line); `chunk_observation` other than absent / false; `memory_type` / `memory_frames` / `memory_stride` (the sparse memory, served by SANA_WAM_SM); `hierarchical_memory` and its pre-rename keys `memory_uniform` / `memory_split` (served by SANA_WAM_HM up to XPolicyLab `d57a617`); a bidirectional class or dataset; the OpenWAM canvas; `sana_pixel_pad: masked`; `rope` other than `aligned`; `use_time_conditioning: true`; dual AttnRes routing.
- **Protocol marker.** The episode protocol has no config key of its own (Sana adds the sink unconditionally since `d37817788`); `window_repeats` is the marker the sink-less recipes carry.

The non-model half (view pixels, prompt rows, normalization, action denormalization) is SANA_WAM's `PolicyInferenceSession`, built on the yaml's **bidirectional view**: the same yaml with the SFT policy class and the 33-frame tier. It resolves exactly as it would for the donor checkpoint (the strip: G = 4 prompt rows, semantic 2x2 view tiles (8, 16); the canvas: G = 1).

**Strict load** (`sana_wam_causal/checkpoint.py`):

- The state dict must carry the donor's trunk, the state token (`state_embed`), and the softmax placement of the config.
- The buffers that SANA_WAM's loader strips are dropped after a shape check. Any other unexpected or missing key is refused.
- The GDN recurrence parameters must be present for **every** GDN block. A checkpoint missing them for only some blocks is refused. `recall_gate` is a buffer the forward never reads and may be absent.

**Normalization** resolves as in SANA_WAM, in this order:

1. an explicit `normalization_path`;
2. `<ckpt_dir>/normalization/<f33 name>`.

The artifact must be the one the yaml pins in `data.extra.robot_sft.normalization_sha256`; for the pantheon33k episode recipes that is `9f8b98ed...` (the donor's), the same statistics as the earlier causal recipes' `1fe3b7e7...`. EEF-only absolute statistics:

- xyz: q01/q99 of both arms pooled;
- rot6d: the fixed [-1, 1] range (identity);
- gripper closedness: [0, 1] mapped to [-1, 1].

The action EEF slots share the state statistics.

**Step-0 pipeline checks.** An SFT checkpoint has no recurrence parameters. With `allow_donor_checkpoint: true`, a dir whose `config.yaml` is the causal yaml and whose `model/` + `normalization/` are the donor's serves the donor as the causal policy at step 0 (on NFS: `/homes/jiamingt/checkpoints/causal_step0/pantheon33k_sana_latent_episode_{videoonly,videoonly_noobs,videoandaction}`). The recurrence parameters are initialized as the causal trainer initializes them on its first load: `gate_proj` zero, and `dt_bias` / `A_log` from the yaml's `dt_bias_init` / `a_log_init` (-5 / 0 for the episode recipes). This gives a decay of about 0.993 per chunk. The ready line then says `gdn_recurrence=initialized (SFT donor, causal step 0)`. Every chunk of such a model reads an untrained memory. Use this for wiring and latency checks only, never as an evaluation.

## Evaluation

```bash
cd XPolicyLab/policy/SANA_WAM_CAUSAL
bash eval.sh <bench_name> <task_name> <ckpt_name> <env_cfg_type> <action_type> <seed> \
  <policy_gpu_id> <env_gpu_id> <policy_conda_env> <eval_env_conda_env>

# offline wiring check, no simulator (needs a GPU for the real model):
EVAL_ENV_TYPE=debug bash eval.sh RoboDojo stack_bowls causal_p33k_episode_vo_stepNNNNN arx_x5 ee 0 0 0 sana_wam base
# same, exercising the server-side image decode path:
EVAL_ENV_TYPE=debug DEBUG_OBS_ENCODED=1 bash eval.sh RoboDojo stack_bowls causal_p33k_episode_vo_stepNNNNN arx_x5 ee 0 0 0 sana_wam base
# simulator, one GPU for both:
bash eval.sh RoboDojo insert_key causal_p33k_episode_vo_stepNNNNN arx_x5 ee 0 0 0 sana_wam robodojo
```

The debug client stops every episode after 20 actions, so it exercises the episode opening (the streams, the sink, the frozen chunk 0, chunk 1) and `reset` only; the commit path needs the simulator. Its fake `*_ee_pose` (all ones) makes the once-per-episode pose check report a gap of about 1.5-1.9 m and warn, which is expected there.

The same conda-activation (`NVCC_PREPEND_FLAGS`) and `pyyaml` notes as in the [SANA_WAM README](../SANA_WAM/README.md#evaluation) apply, as does the parent-directory `env_cfg/` requirement. The adapter checks `get_robot_action_dim_info(env_cfg_type)` against the dual ARX-X5 contract (`arm_dim [6, 6]`, `ee_dim [1, 1]`). `action_type` must be `ee`: the causal line predicts EE poses only.

- One line per `get_action`:

  ```text
  [SANA_WAM_CAUSAL] ep E decision d chunk c: read_chunks=[...] origin=O stream_s=.. commit_s=.. generate_s=.. total_s=.. world_model_rel_rmse(decision d-1)=.. cmd_vs_reached(decision d-1): L pos ../.. mm rot .. deg grip .. | R ...
  ```

  `decision` counts the episode's `get_action` calls from 0; `chunk` is the training chunk the window is (d + 1 for a padded window, whose chunk 0 is the frozen opening; d for a first-frame window). `read_chunks` lists the committed chunks the generation reads besides the sink (the newest `sliding_window_chunks`), `origin` the oldest of them, where the softmax clock of the read starts at 0 (the sink one frame before). The first call of an episode logs `open_stream_s` / `open_commit_s` (the streams started, the sink -- and for a padded window the frozen chunk -- committed) instead of `stream_s` / `commit_s`, which cover the executed chunk. `world_model_rel_rmse` is the relative RMSE between the latent the model predicted for the previous chunk and the streamed latent of what was actually observed. `cmd_vs_reached(decision d-1)` compares the previous chunk's EE commands with the poses reached after them, per arm (`L`/`R`): mean/max position gap in mm, max rotation gap in degrees, max gripper closedness gap.

The RoboDojo client times every RPC out after 120 s whatever `request_timeout_s` says.

## Notes

**Episode cycle.** One `get_action` = one stream encode + one commit + one chunk generation:

1. **First `get_action`: the episode opens at its first frame** (the training's phase window whose chunk grid lands on it).
   1. The three cameras become the view pixels: each stretched to 256x320 and VAE-encoded on its own (an 8x10 latent per view, packed into the 240-token strip) for `sana_latent`, or composited into the 320x512 canvas (a 10x16 latent grid, 160 tokens) for `sana_pixel`.
   2. One causal VAE stream per view starts at that frame: the frame alone gives the **sink** (a `padded` window then also streams the frame repeated over one chunk's 8 frames: the **frozen chunk 0**; a `first_frame` window opens on the sink alone, as Sana's deploy does since `9fca24879`).
   3. `commit_sink` (one clean forward per text stream on video frame 0, no robot rows: the GDN state's first write and the softmax sink), then (padded) the commit of the frozen chunk 0 (under `video_and_action` its rows hold the robot at its first state).
   4. The first emitted chunk (chunk 1 padded, chunk 0 first-frame) is denoised as [its target latent] + [state | 32 actions] (273 tokens on the strip, 193 on the canvas) against the memory, reading its current frame from it (the last frame of the frozen chunk, or the sink).
2. **During a chunk.** RoboDojo calls `update_obs` once after each executed action; the last call arrives right before the next `get_action`. Each call advances the chunk clock tau and keeps the frames of ticks 4, 8, ..., 32 (stride 4). It encodes nothing.
3. **Every later `get_action`.**
   1. Stream the executed chunk c - 1: its 8 frames (ticks 4..32) are encoded on in every view's stream, after everything the stream has seen, which is exactly how training's phase-window encode builds the clean target latent. One clean forward (t = 0) over [that latent] (+ [chunk-start state | executed actions] under `video_and_action`) reads the memory like the generation of that chunk did, then writes the next GDN state and keeps its K/V as the softmax entry of chunk c - 1.
   2. Generate chunk c: 10 Euler steps of [its target latent] + rows against the frozen memory (the GDN state after the commit of chunk c - 1; softmax over the sink, the newest W committed chunks and its own tokens). Its current frame (tick 32 of chunk c - 1) is the last frame of chunk c - 1's latent, already in the memory.
   3. The conditional and unconditional text streams each keep their own memory.
4. **`reset`.** RoboDojo sends `reset` twice per episode; it drops the memory and the streams and is idempotent. An episode that ends mid-chunk commits nothing.

**Refusals.** A chunk executed for fewer than 32 ticks is never committed, because the causal policy never trained on a partial context chunk; the next `get_action` raises. An instruction change inside an episode also raises, since the memory is caption-conditioned.

**Clock.** Under `rope: aligned` the video latents and the robot rows share one physical clock (Sana `_physical_frame_ids` / `forward_sink` / `forward_chunk`):

- the sink is latent frame 0, chunk k's target latent frame k + 1 (chunk 0 at frame 1, frozen or supervised);
- chunk c reads its current frame at latent frame c (chunk c - 1's last target, or the sink for chunk 0);
- its state row sits at step 32c, its actions at steps 32c+1 .. 32c+32.

**Softmax read and positions.**
- Chunk c's forwards (its denoising steps and its commit) read the sink and the newest min(c, 24) committed chunks, c - 24 .. c - 1: training's spans (noisy chunk c reads the sink plus clean chunks max(0, c - W) .. c - 1, clean chunk k the sink plus max(0, k - W) .. k), which is also the default read of Sana's deploy session (W - 1 older chunks plus the most recent one, besides the sink).
- The softmax entries are kept BEFORE the rotary embedding and rotated on the fly at every read, on a clock numbered from 0 at the read's origin O, the oldest chunk read: chunk k in chunk slot k - O, the window of chunk c in slot c - O, and the sink at frame 0, one frame before the earliest frame the read takes. That is the training canvas's **StreamingLLM sink**: a reader whose window evicted older chunks reads the sink right before its window, so the sink is never more than a window away. A read is rotated once per chunk; the 10 denoising steps reuse it.
- This is Sana's deploy `kv_position_mode="rank"` (sink at 0). Sana's default `"absolute"` (sink at the first read frame - 1) differs by one common shift, which the relative RoPE of the aligned clock does not see: the tests match the port against both.
- Under the sink horizon of Sana `0c88c11ab` the origin is O = max(0, c - 7) instead, the chunk at whose start the training canvas reads the sink for chunk c: the read's chunks sit at slots c - W - O .. c - 1 - O and the reader at slot c - O <= 7.
- **GDN layers** are never windowed. Their state keeps keys rotated at the physical positions of the whole episode (the sink at frame 0), and their window runs on that clock.
- **Precision:** GDN numerators use complex64 RoPE; softmax layers use fp64 RoPE cast back to the activation dtype and fp32 attention, as in Sana.

**State and actions** (the D5 contract):

- **Anchor.** Measured joints -> URDF FK -> base-frame link6 pose (xyz + rot6d), and gripper closedness from the observed opening. Joints are masked: EEF-only, 20 active slots, 7-16 and 36-45.
- **Returned actions.** The 32 sampled rows are denormalized as absolute EEF targets. Gripper closedness is clipped to [0, 1]. Each rot6d is replaced by the rotation it encodes (Gram-Schmidt), and the rows are returned as `{left,right}_ee_pose` (link6 in the env-relative world frame, xyz + wxyz) plus `{left,right}_ee_joint_state` (opening).
- **Commit.** These *executed* rows, re-normalized with the same artifact, are what the next commit feeds the model (`video_and_action`). On an unchanged slot they equal the model output to fp32 rounding. The frozen chunk 0's rows are the first measured state held for 32 rows (the training's frozen head: its commands hold the first observed state).

**Train/deploy gaps that remain:**

- The context is the policy's own execution, not demonstrations: the teacher-forcing distribution shift.
- Simulator frames are raw renders, whereas training frames went through x264 (and the phase store was encoded from another mp4 copy than the donor's store).
- The gripper observation is RoboDojo's commanded, rate-limited opening.
- Episodes longer than 49 chunks (fasten_screws: 1,900 steps, 60 chunks) run past the training windows: only the GDN recurrence extrapolates; the softmax read stays at the sink plus 24 chunks.

**Cost per chunk.** Not yet measured on the episode protocol. For reference, the sink-less protocol on the one-view canvas (RTX A6000, bf16, 10 steps, video CFG 6; 2026-10-10) took about 0.27 s for the executed chunk's encode, 0.18 s for the commit (2 forwards), 0.12 s for the observation encode and 1.65-1.75 s for the generation (10 x 2 forwards of 353 tokens). The episode protocol streams 8 new frames per view instead of re-encoding 9, adds one sink commit per episode and encodes no observation; its windows are 273 tokens on the strip and 193 on the canvas. The softmax memory is bf16 pre-RoPE K/V of the sink plus at most 24 chunks per layer and text stream, plus one rotated copy per chunk; the GDN state is per layer.

**Known limitations:**

- Batch-1 sampler (`eval_batch: false`).
- Synchronous deployment only: no asynchronous or speculative chunk generation, no replanning inside a chunk. `n_action_steps < 32` would need one memory per chunk-grid phase (the phases cycle with period 4 at 24 steps); not implemented.
- `rope: aligned` only; the OpenWAM canvas has no causal variant.
- Evaluated so far: the pantheon33k `..._episode_n24_nsc_videoonly_noobs` first-frame checkpoint (epoch_4_step_3324) on all five RoboDojo dimensions, seed 0 (Avg SR 14.75 / score 20.63), through this adapter before the single-form port (XPolicyLab `aad5af3`; the noobs computation is unchanged by it).
